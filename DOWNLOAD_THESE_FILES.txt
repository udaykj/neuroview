=== NEUROVIEW V2 - DOWNLOAD THESE 2 FILES ===

1. StateManagerV2.m - The state management class
2. Either:
   a) neuroView_v2_demo.m - Working demo to test the 4-state system
   OR
   b) CONVERT_TO_V2.m - Script to convert your v1 file automatically

=== HOW TO USE ===

Option A - Try the Demo:
1. Put StateManagerV2.m in your MATLAB folder
2. Put neuroView_v2_demo.m in your MATLAB folder  
3. Run: neuroView_v2_demo()

Option B - Convert Your v1:
1. Put StateManagerV2.m in your MATLAB folder
2. Put CONVERT_TO_V2.m in your MATLAB folder
3. Put your original neuroView.m in the same folder
4. Run: CONVERT_TO_V2('neuroView.m')
5. This creates neuroView_v2.m automatically

=== THAT'S IT! ===

The demo shows the 4-state system working.
The converter automatically updates your v1 code.

Download:
- StateManagerV2.m (required)
- neuroView_v2_demo.m (to test)
OR
- CONVERT_TO_V2.m (to convert your code)
